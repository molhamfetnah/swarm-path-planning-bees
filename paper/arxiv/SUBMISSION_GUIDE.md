# arXiv Submission Guide

## Step 1: Create arXiv Account

1. Go to: https://arxiv.org/user
2. Register with your email
3. Confirm email

---

## Step 2: Prepare Files

Required files (in `paper/arxiv/`):

| File | Status |
|------|--------|
| `submission.tex` | ✅ Ready |
| Convert to PDF | ⬜ Do this |

**To create PDF:**
```bash
# Option 1: If you have LaTeX
pdflatex submission.tex

# Option 2: Use Overleaf
# Upload submission.tex to Overleaf, download PDF
```

---

## Step 3: Upload to arXiv

1. Go to: https://arxiv.org/submit
2. Login
3. Fill in:

**Title:** Modernized Bees Algorithm for Dynamic Path Planning in Robotics

**Authors:** Mulham Fetnah

**Abstract:** (copy from the .tex file)

**Comments:** 6 pages, 3 figures

**Subjects:** cs.RO (Robotics) or cs.AI (Artificial Intelligence)

4. Upload PDF
5. Submit

---

## Step 4: Wait for Processing

- arXiv takes 24-48 hours to process
- You'll get an email with your arXiv ID: `arXiv:YYMM.XXXXX`

---

## After arXiv Approval

1. Get your arXiv ID
2. Update the paper to cite: "Preprint. arXiv:XXXXX"
3. Submit to journal (optional)

---

## Cost: FREE

arXiv is completely free - no fees!

---

## Timeline

| Action | Time |
|--------|------|
| Create account | 5 min |
| Upload PDF | 10 min |
| Processing | 24-48 hours |
| **TOTAL** | ~30 min + waiting |

---

`arXiv:2505.01234`
